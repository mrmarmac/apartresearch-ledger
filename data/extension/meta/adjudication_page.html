This review is not blind. For each item you see the two model answers, their reasons, and what Watchtower says. You give the final decision. Take the time that each item needs.

  • Change: decide the change to the obligation, not to the words. A trigger that is harder to reach is Weakened. A wider exclusion is Weakened. A number that becomes "sufficiently many" is Weakened. If one part is stronger and a different part is weaker, select Weakened.
  • Removal: confirm it only if you do not find the obligation in v2.1. A phrase can move to a different sentence. Open the context when you are not sure.
  • Candidate for a new commitment: select "New commitment" only if the passage commits Meta to something, and v2 has no such obligation. A description of risk is not a commitment. A present-tense statement of practice is a commitment.

The six dimensions of a material change: scope, threshold or trigger, actor, obligation strength (must / will > intend to / aim to > may / consider), disclosure, consequence.
