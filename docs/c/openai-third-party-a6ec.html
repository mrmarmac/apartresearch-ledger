Commitment

Independent expert opinions for evidence produced to SAG: The SAG may opt to get independent expert opinion on the evidence being produced to SAG. The purpose of this input is to add independent analysis from individuals or organizations with deep expertise in domains of relevant risks (e.g. biological risk).
