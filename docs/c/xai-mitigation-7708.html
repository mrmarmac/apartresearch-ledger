Commitment

Driving towards our safety objectives, we continue to design and deploy the following safeguards into our models, as appropriate: ● Safety training: Training our models to recognize and decline harmful requests. ● System prompts: Providing high-priority instructions to our models to enforce our basic refusal policy. ● Filters: Applying classifiers to verify safety when a model is queried regarding topics of CRBN risks
