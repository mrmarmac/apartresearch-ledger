Commitment

For models above the moderate or lower risk threshold in areas where refusal is part of our mitigation strategy, our risk acceptance criteria for external deployment requires evidence demonstrating reliable refusal on benchmark datasets with thorough coverage across relevant threat scenarios. We will research comprehensive mitigations against adversarial attacks, and will evaluate our fully mitigated model under such attacks. The scope of these mitigations, results of validation assessments, and coverage across adversarial attacks will be described in our preparedness reports. As an illustrative example, on the BioTIER refusal evaluation, which includes information that could support development of biological weapons, our risk acceptance criteria includes at least 80% refusal or safe responses, and 40% refusal or safe responses against all adversarial attacks within a typical adversarial attack portfolio.
