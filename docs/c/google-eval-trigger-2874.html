    Before: Where model capabilities remain quite distant from a CCL, a response plan may involve the adoption of additional capability assessment processes to flag when heightened mitigations are required.

    Why this status (source coding): Consequence when capabilities are distant from a CCL: 'adoption of additional capability assessment processes to flag when heightened mitigations are required' becomes 'updating the alert threshold', so the response to a crossed alert threshold may be to move the threshold rather than to add assessment; new condition 'if we assess that risks ... remain acceptable'.
