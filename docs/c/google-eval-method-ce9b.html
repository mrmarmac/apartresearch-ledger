  1. 22 Sep 2025+ AddedFrontier Safety Framework, v3.0
    As a result, to assess the ML R&D CCLs, we may use sources of information about our own progress at accelerating ML R&D to assess whether we are near or at the CCLs, in addition to evaluations of ML R&D capabilities. Similarly, our alert threshold may be defined based on these sources of information, rather than on evaluation scores.

    Why this status (source coding): New: for ML R&D CCLs, proximity and the alert threshold may rest on information about the developer's own ML R&D progress rather than on evaluation scores; no v2.0 provision permits a non-evaluation alert threshold.

    Developer's account: The developer's account identifies the area, but not the change.
