Removed 3

No counterpart in this version.

× Removedgoogle-consequence-c685Consequences of results
A model flagged by an alert threshold may be assessed to pose risks for which readily available mitigations (including but not limited to those described below) may not be sufficient. If this happens, the response plan may involve putting deployment or further development on hold until adequate mitigations can be applied.

No counterpart in this version.

Why this status (source coding): The provision that a response plan 'may involve putting deployment or further development on hold' has no counterpart in v3.0; v3.0 section 1.6 states that the deployment-mitigation criterion 'is required only for external deployment, not further development', and no v3.0 passage contemplates holding development.

Developer's account: The developer published an account. It does not identify this change.

× Removedgoogle-eval-method-1a61Evaluation method
Looking forward, we expect the approach to deceptive alignment risks to take a similar form as the deployment mitigation process. For each deceptive alignment risk, AI developers should: ● Develop a safety case based on the model’s capabilities and the mitigations applied and tested. ... ● Test the mitigations applied to models via red-teaming protocols called “control evaluations,”14 to determine whether they meet the requirements of the safety case.

No counterpart in this version.

Why this status (source coding): The 'Safety Cases and Control Evaluations' subsection, including the expectation of deceptive-alignment safety cases tested by 'control evaluations', has no counterpart; 'control evaluation' does not occur in v3.0 and Section 4 contains no safety-case or testing provision for misalignment.

Developer's account: The developer's account identifies the area, but not the change.

No counterpart in this version.

Why this status (source coding): No v3.0 CCL addresses automation of end-to-end attacks on organisations with limited security posture; v3.0 Table 2.2.2.a has a single 'Cyber uplift level 1' CCL.

↓ Weakenedgoogle-consequence-3b1dConsequences of results
Post-deployment processes: our safety cases and mitigations may be updated if deemed necessary by post-market monitoring. Material updates to a safety case will be submitted to the appropriate governance function for review.

Before: Post-deployment review of safety case: the safety case will be updated through red-teaming and revisions to our threat models. The safeguards for the model may be updated as well to ensure continued adequacy.

Why this status (source coding): Obligation strength: 'the safety case will be updated through red-teaming and revisions to our threat models' becomes 'safety cases and mitigations may be updated if deemed necessary'; method anchors (red-teaming, threat model revisions) replaced by 'post-market monitoring'.

Developer's account: The developer published an account. It does not identify this change.

↓ Weakenedgoogle-consequence-6541Consequences of results
Pre-deployment review of safety case: general availability deployment8 external deployments of a model takes take place only after the appropriate corporate governance body function determines the safety case regarding each CCL the model has reached to be adequate. In particular, we will deem deployment mitigations adequate if the evidence suggests that for the CCLs the model has reached, the increase in likelihood of severe harm has been reduced to an acceptable level.

Mixed change. Stronger in one way and weaker in another. The status is then Weakened.

Why this status (source coding): Scope: gate moves from 'general availability deployment' (footnote 8: public production deployments without significant restrictions) to 'external deployments', a broader class; an adequacy standard ('reduced to an acceptable level') is added; 'takes place only after' retained.

Developer's account: The developer's account identifies the area, but not the change.

↓ Weakenedgoogle-eval-method-c6b4Evaluation method
The assessment takes the form of a safety case, and could take into account factors such as: ... What capabilities and mitigations are available on other publicly available models. For example, whether another (non-Google) publicly deployed model is at the same CCL, and has mitigations that are less effective at preventing misuse than that of the model being assessed, in which case the deployment of this model is less likely to materially increase risk.

Before: Assessing the robustness of these mitigations against the risk posed through assurance evaluations and threat modeling research. The assessment takes the form of a safety case, taking into account factors such as the likelihood and consequences of misuse.

Developer's account: The developer published an account. It does not identify this change.

↓ Weakenedgoogle-security-8b81Security
These recommended security levels reflect our current thinking proportionate to the risks posed and may be adjusted if our empirical understanding of the risks changes. This may occur if, for example, a model does not possess capabilities meaningfully different from other widely publicly available models that have not demonstrably caused or contributed to severe risks, weaker security applied (in which case the marginal benefit of higher security is limited), or if we assess that the benefits of the open release of model weights outweigh the risks.

Why this status (source coding): Consequence: the condition for adjusting a security level changes from comparable models 'that have not demonstrably caused or contributed to severe risks' to comparable models 'that have weaker security applied', so a competitor's weaker security now suffices; v3.0 1.6 adds a further override ('or we otherwise assess that the level of security applied is adequate, e.g. if they match or exceed the level of security applied to other models with similar capabilities').

Strengthened 7

↑ Strengthenedgoogle-consequence-b892Consequences of results
The following deployment mitigation process for external deployments will be applied to models reaching a misuse CCL, allowing for iterative and flexible tailoring of mitigations to each risk and use case. ... The following mitigation process will be applied for deployments of a model reaching a ML R&D CCL. The approach is similar to misuse deployment mitigations, with an added focus on large scale internal deployments.

Why this status (source coding): Scope: the process now also covers 'large scale internal deployments' for models reaching ML R&D CCLs (v2.0 process covered deployments only); for misuse CCLs the process is now stated 'for external deployments'.

↑ Strengthenedgoogle-eval-scope-b23dEvaluation scope
We describe three sets of CCLs: misuse CCLs, machine learning R&D CCLs, and misalignment CCLs. For misuse risk, we define CCLs in the following risk domains where the misuse of model capabilities may result in severe harm: ● CBRN: Risks of models assisting in the development, preparation, and/or execution of a chemical, biological, radiological, or nuclear (“CBRN”) threat. ● Cyber: ... ● Harmful Manipulation: Risks of models with high manipulative capabilities potentially being misused...

Before: For misuse risk, we define CCLs in high-risk domains where, based on early research, we believe risks of severe harm may be most likely to arise from future models: ● CBRN: Risks of models assisting in the development, preparation, and/or execution of a chemical, biological, radiological, or nuclear (“CBRN”) attack. ● Cyber: Risks of models assisting in the development, preparation, and/or…

Why this status (source coding): Scope of risk domains broadened: 'Harmful Manipulation' added as a misuse domain; CBRN, Cyber and ML R&D retained (ML R&D moved out of 'misuse' into its own set, a reorganisation); 'deceptive alignment' relabelled 'misalignment'.

Developer's account: The developer's account identifies this change.

↑ Strengthenedgoogle-eval-trigger-0e13Triggers and thresholds
For each risk domain, we conduct aspects of our risk assessment at various moments throughout the model development process, both before and after deployment. We conduct a risk assessment for the first external deployment of a new frontier AI model. For subsequent versions of the model, we conduct a further risk assessment if the model has meaningful new capabilities or a material increase in performance, until the model is retired or we deploy a more capable model.

Before: We intend to evaluate our most powerful frontier models regularly to check whether their AI capabilities are approaching a CCL. We also intend to evaluate any of these models that could indicate an exceptional increase in capabilities over previous models, and where appropriate, assess the likelihood of such capabilities and risks before and during training.

Why this status (source coding): Trigger: 'regularly' and 'exceptional increase in capabilities' replaced by 'first external deployment' plus 'meaningful new capabilities or a material increase in performance' (a lower bar than 'exceptional'); modal: 'intend to evaluate' to 'we conduct'.

Developer's account: The developer's account identifies the area, but not the change.
